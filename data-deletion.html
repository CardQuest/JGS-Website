Data Deletion Instructions

Radiant Clash by JUDGE GAMES STUDIO  ·  Last updated 18 November 2026

1. Overview

Radiant Clash (previously Card Quest) is developed by JUDGE GAMES STUDIO. It is available on Facebook Instant Games, as an Android app on Google Play, and on other platforms. You can delete your account and the personal data we hold about you at any time. This page explains the ways to do that, what gets deleted, and what we keep.

  • Android app: use Option A (in the game), or Option C (email) if you no longer have the app.
  • Facebook: use Option B or Option C.
  • Any other platform: use Option C.

2. Option A — Delete in the Game (Android App)

This is the fastest method. Deletion happens immediately.

  1. Open Radiant Clash on your Android device.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, then tap DELETE FOREVER to confirm.
Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on every device. If you open the game again later, it starts a brand-new account.

3. Option B — Remove the App from Facebook

If you play on Facebook, this sends an automatic deletion request from Facebook to our servers. You don’t need to email us or wait.

  1. Open Facebook (web, mobile app, or Messenger).
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  3. Select Apps and Websites from the left-hand menu (web) or under Account Settings (mobile).
  4. Find Radiant Clash (or Card Quest, its previous name) in the list of active apps and tap Remove.
  5. Confirm the removal. Facebook will notify our servers automatically.
Once Facebook notifies our servers, your account will be anonymised within minutes. You will receive a confirmation code that you can use to track the request status if needed.

4. Option C — Email Request (Any Platform)

Use this if you can’t use the options above. For example, you may have uninstalled the Android app, or you may want to confirm that a deletion went through. You don’t need the game installed.

Send an email to support.cardquest@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Removal Request” and include:

  • Your in-game display name
  • Your Help ID (found at the bottom of the in-game Settings panel), if you can still open the game
  • The platform you play on (for example Android or Facebook)
  • A short confirmation that you are requesting deletion of your account

If you no longer have the game, tell us your player name and roughly when you last played. We will use that to find your account, and we may ask a follow-up question to confirm it is yours.

We will process the request within 30 days and reply once deletion is complete.

5. What Gets Deleted

Every option deletes the same things. The following data is removed:

  • Every way to log in to the account: your Facebook or other platform ID link, your Google Play Games link, and the account key stored for your Android device
  • All login sessions (you are signed out everywhere)
  • Your rating and leaderboard placement
  • In-game mail and friend links
  • Bug reports you sent, including screenshots
  • Device and loading logs and tutorial activity logs

Your profile is anonymised. Your name becomes “Deleted Player” and your photo and friend code are removed. Open marketplace listings and pending trades are cancelled.

Your cards, decks, currencies (including Jade) and progress cannot be recovered. The account can never be logged in to again.

6. What We Keep

Some limited data is kept after deletion. It is no longer linked to your name, photo, or any login:

  • Purchase transaction records (transaction ID, item, amount, date) for up to 7 years, for refunds, tax and legal compliance.
  • Records of battles and completed trades or sales that involved other players, so their history stays correct. These show the player as “Deleted Player”.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying gameplay statistics used for balance and analytics.

Google keeps its own Google Play Games data, such as your Play Games profile. You can manage or delete that in your Google account settings. Deleting your Radiant Clash account removes only the link to it.

7. Timing

  • Option A (in the game): immediate.
  • Option B (Facebook removal): automatic, usually within a few minutes of Facebook notifying our servers.
  • Option C (email): processed within 30 days of receipt. We reply once deletion is complete.

8. Questions

If you have any questions about the deletion process or want to confirm the status of a previous request, contact us at support.cardquest@gmail.com.
