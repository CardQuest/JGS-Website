Privacy Policy

JUDGE GAMES STUDIO  ·  Last updated 18 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how JUDGE GAMES STUDIO (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and handles your information when you play Radiant Clash, on every platform where we publish it. That includes Facebook Instant Games, the Radiant Clash Android app on Google Play, and other platforms we may release on. By playing Radiant Clash, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Radiant Clash was previously published as Card Quest. This policy covers both names; your account and data carry over.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Login Information

How you log in depends on where you play. We receive only what we need to identify your account:

  • Facebook Instant Games: with your permission, your Facebook Player ID (a unique identifier), display name, and profile photo URL.
  • Android app: the app creates a random account key and keeps it on your device. We store only a one-way hash of that key. If you sign in to Google Play Games, we also receive your Play Games player ID, so your progress is kept when you reinstall or change phones. We do not receive your Google account email, name, or photo.
  • Other platforms: the unique player ID that platform provides, and your name there if the platform shares it.

You can also choose a player name in the game. Accounts on different platforms are separate unless we tell you otherwise in the game.

2.2 Gameplay Data

We collect data generated by your use of the game, including:

  • Game progress, ratings, and match history
  • In-game currency balances (coins, crystals, jade)
  • Card collection and deck configurations
  • Region selection and matchmaking preferences
  • Quest and milestone progress
  • Session timestamps and activity logs
  • Friend links and in-game mail

2.3 Device and Diagnostic Data

To keep the game running and fix problems, we collect:

  • Device and app details, such as device or browser type, operating system, language, app version, and a random device identifier used to measure loading problems
  • Crash and error reports
  • Bug reports you choose to send from the game, including any screenshot you attach

2.4 Purchase Data

Radiant Clash offers in-app purchases through the following channels:

  • Facebook (Meta Instant Games): payments are processed by Meta Platforms, Inc. under Meta's payment terms. We receive only a payment ID, the item purchased, the amount and currency charged, and confirmation that the payment succeeded. We use these to grant your Jade and to prevent duplicate or fraudulent purchases.
  • iOS (Apple App Store): payments are processed by Apple. We receive only a transaction ID, the item purchased, and the jade amount granted. We never see your payment details.
  • Android (Google Play): payments are processed by Google under the same terms as above.
  • Web Store (Stripe): web purchases are processed by Stripe, Inc. Depending on your chosen payment method (such as credit card, Alipay, UPI, GrabPay, or other regional methods), Stripe may collect your payment method details directly. We do not receive or store your card number, bank account details, or other sensitive payment information. We receive only a Stripe transaction ID, the payment method type used (e.g. “card”, “alipay”), the amount paid, and confirmation of success.

Stripe’s privacy practices are governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy, available at stripe.com/privacy.

2.5 Advertising

Radiant Clash may show optional rewarded video ads and banner ads. The ad provider depends on the platform. On Facebook it is Meta Audience Network. On other platforms, including Android, we may use a third-party ad network. Ad networks may collect device and usage information, such as an advertising identifier, IP address, and how you interact with ads, to serve, measure, and in some cases personalise ads. They do this under their own privacy policies.

We receive only confirmation that a rewarded ad was completed, so we can grant the reward. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your game account
  • Save and synchronise your game progress across sessions and devices
  • Enable multiplayer features including PvP matchmaking and raids
  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Operate leaderboards and seasonal rating systems
  • Detect and prevent cheating, fraud, or abuse
  • Improve gameplay balance and fix bugs

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

  • With other players: your display name, profile photo, rating, and region may be visible to other players during matchmaking and on leaderboards.
  • With service providers: we use third-party services to operate the game. These include server and database hosting, network and security services (Cloudflare), error monitoring (Sentry), sign-in services (Google Play Games and the platform you play on), payment processors, and ad networks (see 2.5). These providers access data only as necessary to provide their services and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Purchase transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years for accounting and legal compliance purposes.

5.1 Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and its data at any time:

  • In the game (Android app): open Settings and tap Delete account at the bottom. Deletion happens immediately.
  • On Facebook: remove Radiant Clash from your Facebook Apps and Websites settings. Facebook then tells our servers to delete your account automatically.
  • By email, from any platform: contact us at the address below. You can use this even if you no longer have the game installed.

Full steps, what is deleted, and what is kept are on our Data Deletion page.

6. Children’s Privacy

Radiant Clash is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), login keys are stored only as one-way hashes, and every transaction is validated on our servers. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this document. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
