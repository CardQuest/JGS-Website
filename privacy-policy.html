Privacy Policy

JUDGE GAMES STUDIO  ·  Last updated 21 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how JUDGE GAMES STUDIO (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and handles your information when you play the games we publish on Facebook Instant Games, and on the other platforms where we release them. By playing any of our games, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Where a practice applies to only one game, this policy names that game. Everything else applies to all of our games.

1.1 Our Games

  • Radiant Clash, a collectible card battler. It is available on Facebook Instant Games, as an Android app on Google Play, and on other platforms we may release on. Radiant Clash was previously published as Card Quest. This policy covers both names; your account and data carry over.
  • Hoop It!, a basketball word game on Facebook Instant Games. Hoop It! has no servers of its own. Its data is held by Facebook as part of Instant Games, with a local copy on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Login Information

How you log in depends on the game and where you play. We receive only what we need to identify your account:

  • Facebook Instant Games (all our games): through the Facebook Instant Games SDK, your Facebook Player ID (a unique identifier), display name, and profile photo.
  • Radiant Clash Android app: the app creates a random account key and keeps it on your device. We store only a one-way hash of that key. If you sign in to Google Play Games, we also receive your Play Games player ID, so your progress is kept when you reinstall or change phones. We do not receive your Google account email, name, or photo.
  • Radiant Clash on other platforms: the unique player ID that platform provides, and your name there if the platform shares it.

In Radiant Clash you can also choose a player name. Radiant Clash accounts on different platforms are separate unless we tell you otherwise in the game. Each of our games keeps its own account; they are not linked to each other.

2.2 Gameplay Data

We collect data generated by your use of our games.

Radiant Clash stores the following on our servers:

  • Game progress, ratings, and match history
  • In-game currency balances (coins, crystals, jade)
  • Card collection and deck configurations
  • Region selection and matchmaking preferences
  • Quest and milestone progress
  • Session timestamps and activity logs
  • Friend links and in-game mail

Hoop It! stores:

  • Game progress and settings (coins, hints, streaks, achievements, purchases you own, and preferences). These are saved in Facebook’s Instant Games cloud storage, with a local copy in your browser’s local storage on your device.
  • Your scores, on Facebook’s Instant Games leaderboards (daily and endless, for each difficulty) and in Facebook tournaments.

2.3 Friends, Challenges, and Invites (Hoop It!)

Hoop It! shows your display name and profile photo on its leaderboards, in friend challenges and tournaments, and in share and invite messages you choose to send. When a player joins Hoop It! through a friend’s invite link, the game records the inviter’s player ID so the inviter can be rewarded. Like the rest of Hoop It!’s data, this record is kept in Facebook’s Instant Games services, not on servers of ours.

2.4 Device, Diagnostic, and Analytics Data

Radiant Clash: to keep the game running and fix problems, we collect:

  • Device and app details, such as device or browser type, operating system, language, app version, and a random device identifier used to measure loading problems
  • Crash and error reports
  • Bug reports you choose to send from the game, including any screenshot you attach

Hoop It!: we use Meta App Events to record gameplay events, such as levels played and purchases made. We use this to improve the game.

2.5 Purchase Data

Our games offer in-app purchases. Radiant Clash sells Jade. Hoop It! sells ad removal, coin packs, and a starter pack. Purchases go through the following channels:

  • Facebook (Meta Instant Games), all our games: payments are processed by Meta Platforms, Inc. under Meta's payment terms, through Facebook Game Payments. We receive only a purchase confirmation, such as a payment ID, the item purchased, the amount and currency charged, and confirmation that the payment succeeded. We never receive your card or payment details. We use these to give you what you bought (for example Jade in Radiant Clash, or ad removal and coins in Hoop It!) and to prevent duplicate or fraudulent purchases.
  • iOS (Apple App Store), Radiant Clash: payments are processed by Apple. We receive only a transaction ID, the item purchased, and the jade amount granted. We never see your payment details.
  • Android (Google Play), Radiant Clash: payments are processed by Google under the same terms as above.
  • Web Store (Stripe), Radiant Clash: web purchases are processed by Stripe, Inc. Depending on your chosen payment method (such as credit card, Alipay, UPI, GrabPay, or other regional methods), Stripe may collect your payment method details directly. We do not receive or store your card number, bank account details, or other sensitive payment information. We receive only a Stripe transaction ID, the payment method type used (e.g. “card”, “alipay”), the amount paid, and confirmation of success.

Stripe’s privacy practices are governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy, available at stripe.com/privacy.

2.6 Advertising

Our games show ads. Radiant Clash may show optional rewarded video ads and banner ads. Hoop It! shows interstitial, rewarded, and banner ads. The ad provider depends on the platform. On Facebook it is Meta Audience Network, for all our games. On other platforms, including Android, Radiant Clash may use a third-party ad network. Ad networks may collect device and usage information, such as an advertising identifier, IP address, and how you interact with ads, to serve, measure, and in some cases personalise ads. They do this under their own privacy policies.

We receive only confirmation that a rewarded ad was completed, so we can grant the reward. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings.

2.7 What We Do Not Collect

Hoop It! does not collect your email address, phone number, location, or contacts. It does not use Facebook Login or the Facebook Graph API to read data from your Facebook account. We do not sell data from any of our games.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your game account
  • Save and synchronise your game progress across sessions and devices
  • Enable multiplayer features, including PvP matchmaking and raids in Radiant Clash, and friend challenges and tournaments in Hoop It!
  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Operate leaderboards, including Radiant Clash’s seasonal rating system and Hoop It!’s Facebook leaderboards
  • Reward players who invite friends (Hoop It!)
  • Show ads and grant rewards for watching them
  • Detect and prevent cheating, fraud, or abuse
  • Improve gameplay balance and fix bugs

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

  • With other players: your display name and profile photo may be visible to other players. In Radiant Clash, so may your rating and region, during matchmaking and on leaderboards. In Hoop It!, they appear on leaderboards, in friend challenges and tournaments, and in share and invite messages you send.
  • With service providers: we use third-party services to operate our games. For all our games, these include Meta (Instant Games, Game Payments, and Audience Network). For Hoop It!, Meta also provides cloud saves, leaderboards, tournaments, and App Events analytics. For Radiant Clash, they also include server and database hosting, network and security services (Cloudflare), error monitoring (Sentry), sign-in services (Google Play Games and the platform you play on), other payment processors, and ad networks (see 2.6). These providers access data only as necessary to provide their services and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Retention

For Radiant Clash, we retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Purchase transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years for accounting and legal compliance purposes.

Hoop It! keeps no data on servers of ours. Its progress, purchases, scores, and referral records are held by Facebook as part of Instant Games, under Meta’s policies. The local copy stays in your browser until you clear its data for the game.

5.1 Deleting Your Data

You can ask us to delete your data from any of our games at any time:

  • In the game (Radiant Clash Android app): open Settings and tap Delete account at the bottom. Deletion happens immediately.
  • On Facebook: remove the game from your Facebook Apps and Websites settings. For Radiant Clash, Facebook then tells our servers to delete your account automatically. For Hoop It!, this removes the game’s access, and Facebook handles the game data it holds under Meta’s policies.
  • By email, for any game and any platform: contact us at the address below and tell us which game or games your request is for. You can use this even if you no longer have the game.

Full steps, what is deleted, and what is kept are on our Data Deletion page.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). In Radiant Clash, login keys are stored only as one-way hashes, and every transaction is validated on our servers. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us and tell us which game or games your request is about. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this document. Continued use of our games after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
